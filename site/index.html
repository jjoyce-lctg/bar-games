How it works

Three steps, one round

No app, no account. Everything happens from the code on the TV.

1

Scan the code

When the DJ opens a game, a QR code goes up on the TVs. Point your phone's camera at it.

2

Pick your racer

Enter your name and mobile number and choose a mascot or marble. Then watch the big screen.

3

Win a round

If your racer wins, your prize is texted to you. Redeem it from the next day at any Last Call Tavern Group bar, for 14 days.

The games

Fast, loud, and over in a minute

Each game is built for a packed bar: quick to join, easy to follow from across the room, and done before your next drink arrives.

Playing now

Mascot Race

Four mascots, one track. Back a racer and cheer it to the line. Everyone who picked the winner takes home the prize.

Playing now

Bar Marbles

Your own marble, tagged with your initials, dropped into a 3D course with a tornado, a plinko board and a knock-off ledge. First five across the line place; first across wins.

Prizes

A different prize every night

Tonight's prize is on the TV before each game. Every game night has something on the line, and it changes from night to night. Winners get a text, and the prize is good at any of our bars.

  • Redeem from the day after the game, for 14 days
  • Just give a staff member your phone number
  • One entry per phone number per game, must be 21+ to claim
